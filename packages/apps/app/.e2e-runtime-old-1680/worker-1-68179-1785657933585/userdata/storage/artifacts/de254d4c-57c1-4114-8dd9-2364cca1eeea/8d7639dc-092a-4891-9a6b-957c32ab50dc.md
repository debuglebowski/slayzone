# Peka — Level Design: Act 1 (Teach)

> Companion to `game-design.md`. Concrete puzzle sketches for the opening act.
> Goal of Act 1: teach **flip**, then each co-op verb, ending on a combined test.
> Every puzzle is **impossible solo** by design (after the brief intro).

**Reading the diagrams**
```
═══ ceiling surface (gravity "up")        P = a player
        · · · lethal gap / void · · ·     ▲ = flip up    ▼ = flip down
═══ floor surface (gravity "down")        ▒ = shadow creature (lethal)
```
Players are symmetric — "P1 / P2" just label who acts; either gamepad works.

---

## 0. Intro — "Two ways to fall" *(safe, parallel solo-teach)*

Teaches the **flip** with zero death risk. Floor and ceiling are close; the gap
is shallow. Each player independently flips to pass a small obstacle on their
surface.

```
═══════╗      ╔═══════════
       ║  P2▲ ║                ceiling: P2 must flip down past a stub
       ╚══════╝
       (narrow, safe)
═══════╗      ╔═══════════
       ║  P1▼ ║                floor:  P1 must flip up past a stub
═══════╝      ╚═══════════
```
- **Teaches:** the flip button; you can ride either surface; mid-air feels safe here.
- **Solo?** Yes — intentional tutorial. First and only soloable beat.

---

## 1. "Two at once" — teach **simultaneity** (hold-two)

The exit gate latches open **only if a floor-plate and a ceiling-plate are
pressed at the same instant**. They're far apart — one on each surface. One body
can't occupy both → needs two players.

```
═════════[Bᵒ]══════════════════════  ceiling plate (B)
                                 ║GATE║
═══════════════════[Aᵒ]═════════║    ║  floor plate (A) → exit
```
- **Solve:** P2 flips to ceiling, stands on B; P1 stands on A; on a shared count
  the gate latches open; both proceed.
- **Impossible solo:** simultaneous press, two surfaces, one body.
- **Teaches:** simultaneity + "flip to reach the other surface."
- **Twist later:** plates that must be *held* (not latch) → forces clever timing.

---

## 2. "Be the bridge" — teach **bodies-as-platforms** + **asymmetric reach**

A lethal gap. The ceiling above it is spiked, so you can't just go over. A plate
extends a thin ledge into the gap **only while pressed** — and the presser can't
also cross. Reciprocal: the far side has a plate to send a ledge back.

```
═══════════▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒════════  spiked ceiling over the gap (no go-over)
            · · · gap · · ·
═════[P2 on plate]──ledge──   · · ·   ──ledge──[plate]═══ exit
```
- **Solve:** P2 holds the near plate → ledge extends → P1 crosses → P1 hits the
  far plate → ledge extends back → P2 crosses.
- **Impossible solo:** someone must hold while someone crosses.
- **Teaches:** take turns being the other's path; your reach ≠ your own benefit.

---

## 3. "Patrol" — introduce the **lethal shadow creature** (timing)

A creature patrols P1's floor stretch (instant death). P1 can't pass on its own
schedule. A ceiling switch (reachable only by the player up top) opens a timed
alcove / drops a shutter that stalls the creature — but the switch-holder is the
*other* player.

```
═══════[Sᵒ ceiling switch]══════════════════
                          (controls floor below)
══════════════▒→→→→→▒══════════[alcove]═══ P1 path
```
- **Solve:** P2 (ceiling) times the switch to the creature's patrol so P1 slips
  through / hides; then they swap roles for a creature on the ceiling ahead.
- **Impossible solo:** you can't watch+time your own threat and walk it.
- **Teaches:** threats are surface-bound; your partner manages *your* danger;
  instant-death stakes; reading enemy patterns together.

---

## 4. "The drop" — **capstone**: flip-redirect crossing under pressure

Combines everything. A vertical shaft: to descend safely you must **flip-jump**
between staggered ledges, and several landings are **plate-held platforms** the
partner extends just-in-time from the opposite surface — while a creature drifts
the shaft.

```
═══╗                 ╔════  P2 holds plate → platform [▭] appears mid-shaft
   ║   [▭]?   ▒       ║
   ║         (drifts) ║
   ║   [▭]?           ║
═══╝                 ╚════  P1 flip-jumps down ledge→[▭]→ledge, timing the creature
```
- **Solve:** P2 reads P1's descent and extends each platform a beat before P1
  arrives, dodging the creature; roles reverse for P2's descent.
- **Impossible solo:** mid-air you can't also be holding the platform you need.
- **Teaches:** mid-air flip-redirect as traversal + trust/timing + all prior verbs.
- **Act 1 ends** by depositing the pair at the **nadir** — the lowest point, where
  the split happened. From here every act climbs *up* toward the light. (No
  checkpoints — death restarts the whole run, per GDD §3.)

## Teaching curve (summary)

| # | Verb taught | Forcing function |
|---|-------------|------------------|
| 0 | Flip | (tutorial, soloable) |
| 1 | Simultaneity | two plates, one body |
| 2 | Bodies-as-platforms / reach | hold-while-cross |
| 3 | Threat timing | manage partner's danger |
| 4 | Flip-redirect + combine | mid-air can't self-support |
