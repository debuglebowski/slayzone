## 2. Content corrections (7) — before → after

| # | Section | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Least Privilege | roles "Data Scientist, Model Trainer, Admin" | "User, Admin, Credit Manager" | Real Better-Auth roles; ML roles don't exist |
| 2 | Separate Duties | "data preparation, model deployment, security auditing" | "development, deployment/release, security review" | Reflects actual SDLC, not an ML pipeline |
| 3 | Secure Defaults | "Use two-factor authentication (2FA) for administrators" | "Require SSO for administrators and enable MFA at the identity provider" | App has no native 2FA; auth is SSO (Entra/Google). Avoids a testable policy-vs-practice gap |
| 4 | Privacy / Full Functionality | "differential privacy techniques for personalized recommendations" | "Minimize personal data shared with third-party AI providers; aggregate or redact where feasible" | Normain calls third-party LLMs (Azure OpenAI); does not train models |
| 5 | Privacy / Full Functionality | "models that operate on aggregated or anonymized data" | "Prefer aggregated or anonymized inputs when sending data to external AI services" | Same — inference, not model training |
| 6 | Correct Flaws Promptly | "dedicated team to investigate and patch" | "Assign clear responsibility for investigating and patching flaws promptly" | No dedicated team in a 6-person org |
| 7 | Transparency | "Offer users a dashboard to view and manage their data" | "Provide users a means to access and manage their data on request" | No self-service dashboard exists; deletion/access is via runbook on request |

All 7 confirmed applied to the live doc (occurrencesChanged = 1 each); all 7 confirmed absent from the archive.
