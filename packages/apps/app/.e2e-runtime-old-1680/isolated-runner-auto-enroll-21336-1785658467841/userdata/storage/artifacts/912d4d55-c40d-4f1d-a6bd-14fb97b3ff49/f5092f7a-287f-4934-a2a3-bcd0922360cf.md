# UC03 — Regulatory-vs-Policy Gap Check

**Mode**: Spreadsheet (1 template × 15 obligation rows)
**Persona**: GRC / internal audit lead
**Scenario**: Given a regulation (NIS2 Directive) and Normain's internal security policies, enumerate the obligations and assess whether the policies address each (Yes / Partial / No), with citations on both sides and rationale.

## Summary metrics

- **Coverage profile**: 1 Yes / 5 Partial / 9 No → realistic for a young policy stack focused on secure ops + secure dev; gaps are concentrated in regulator-facing obligations (notification timelines, management oversight, crypto, MFA, supply chain) and in adjacent policies that aren't in scope (BCP, third-party, HR).
- **Expected competitor difficulty**: high. Citation fidelity will be the differentiator — competitors often hallucinate that "this is partially addressed in §X" when §X doesn't exist or doesn't say what they claim.
- **Hard-failure traps**: fabricated section names; misattributing supply-chain security to the in-scope policies (delegated reference); marking BCP as "addressed" because backup is mentioned (backup ≠ DR/crisis management).
