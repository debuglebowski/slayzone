# Identifying the Right People

> **Are you the owner of your Azure tenant?** If so, you likely have all the permissions needed and can handle every step yourself. The sections below are for organizations where different people manage billing, subscriptions, and IT.

To complete the Marketplace offer process, you may need people with different permissions in your organization. This guide helps you find out who can handle each step.

All steps below start from the [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com).

## Do You Have a Suitable Azure Subscription?

An active Azure subscription linked to your billing account is required to complete the purchase. **Not all subscription types work** — the subscription must have a valid payment method. Subscriptions like MSDN, Visual Studio, free trials, or sponsorship subscriptions cannot be used for Marketplace purchases.

**How to check:**

1. In the Azure Portal, search for **Subscriptions** and open it
2. Check if you have a subscription listed with a status of **Active**
3. Make sure it has a valid payment method (check under **Cost Management + Billing** → your subscription → **Payment methods**)

> ### Don't have a suitable subscription?
>
> Someone in your organization will need to create one first:
>
> - **Enterprise Agreement** — Enterprise Administrator or Account Owner
> - **Microsoft Customer Agreement** — Owner or Contributor on the billing account or billing profile
> - **Pay-as-you-go** — the account holder
>
> **How to create one:**
>
> 1. In the Azure Portal, search for **Subscriptions** and open it
> 2. Click **+ Add** and follow the steps
> 3. Name it something recognizable, e.g. `Normain` or `Normain-<plan-name>`

## Who Can Accept the Offer?

This person needs billing admin permissions.

**How to check:**

1. In the Azure Portal, search for **Cost Management + Billing** and open it
2. Click **Access control (IAM)** in the left menu
3. Look for users with one of these roles:
   - **Enterprise Administrator** (Enterprise Agreement)
   - **Billing account owner** or **contributor** (Microsoft Customer Agreement)
   - **Account Administrator** (pay-as-you-go)

## Who Can Complete the Purchase?

This person needs permissions on the Azure subscription that will be used for the purchase.

**How to check:**

1. In the Azure Portal, search for **Subscriptions** and open it
2. Select the subscription you plan to use
3. Click **Access control (IAM)** → **Role assignments**
4. Look for users with **Owner** or **Contributor** role

## Who Can Approve App Access?

This is only needed when signing in to the application after setup — not during the Marketplace purchase itself. If your organization requires admin approval for new apps, this person needs IT administrator permissions.

**How to check:**

1. In the Azure Portal, search for **Microsoft Entra ID** (or **Azure Active Directory**) and open it
2. Click **Roles and administrators**
3. Look for users with the **Global Administrator** role
