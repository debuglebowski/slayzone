## Summary

Internal operator runbook for offboarding an enterprise customer who purchased Normain via Microsoft Marketplace under a Private Offer. Triggered when Sales and the customer have decided to end the relationship.

Supersedes the legacy *Runbook - Delete customer data* for Marketplace customers (which targets the old single-DB architecture).

## Sections

1. Triggers and inputs
2. Architecture recap (what gets removed)
3. What the Marketplace webhook does (and does not do) — Renew / Suspend / Unsubscribe / Reinstate are status-only updates; no infra change
4. Pre-flight checks (CRM, contract, legal hold, DB state)
5. Customer notification and data return
6. Cancel the Marketplace subscription (Partner Center → webhook verification)
7. Suspend service (T+0) — manual `kubectl scale --replicas=0` since webhook does not block traffic
8. Retention period (T+0 to T+90 days, per Data Management Policy)
9. Decommission (T+90 days) — final snapshot, drop DB, delete storage/PV, remove from tenant registry, terraform destroy via `provision-tenants.yml`
10. Evidence package
11. Closure
12. Roles and responsibilities
13. Roll-back (only feasible during retention window)
14. Related artifacts
15. Review cadence

## Key compliance hooks

- **Retention:** 90 days from contract termination (Data Management Policy), unless contract specifies otherwise.
- **Critical safeguards in code:** `prevent_destroy = true` on per-tenant Postgres DB; PV `reclaim_policy = Retain` on data-sources volume — both must be handled deliberately during step 9.
- **Webhook contract source:** `apps/server/tenant-api/src/marketplace/landing-router.ts` (handles Renew / Suspend / Unsubscribe / Reinstate).
- **Provisioning workflow:** `.github/workflows/provision-tenants.yml` reads tenants from `tenant.normain.com/v1/api/tenants/get-all` → drives terraform destroy when a slug is removed.
