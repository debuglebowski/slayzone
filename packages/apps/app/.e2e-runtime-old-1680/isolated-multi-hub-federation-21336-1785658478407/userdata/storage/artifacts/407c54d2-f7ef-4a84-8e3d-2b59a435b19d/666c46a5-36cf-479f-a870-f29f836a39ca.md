# UC01 — Questions + Ground-Truth Answers

15 distinct questions asked on the same corpus (Simple mode). Each question is asked verbatim across all competitors. Ground truth authored from a direct read of both `.htm` documents.

## How competitors are asked

- Upload `msft-fy24-10k.htm` and `msft-fy24-proxy.htm` into a fresh session/project.
- For each question below, paste the question text verbatim. No system prompt, no preamble.
- Capture: prompt, full answer text, full-session PDF, screenshot, anomaly notes.

## Trap rows (catch fabricated answers)

- **Q14** — competitors may invent an Activision impairment number. Correct: "not described in the document."
- **Q15** — competitors may overstate by claiming a dedicated "nation-state attacks" risk factor category. Correct: sub-topic within broader cyber category.
