### What's actually missing for an ISO auditor
The mapped tests are proxies. A.7.14 evidence an auditor expects:
1. **Documented procedure** describing verification of data/software removal before disposal or re-use → covered by Asset Mgmt Policy + Data Mgmt Policy (PDFs already in Vanta)
2. **Execution evidence** for the audit period — a log of devices disposed/re-used + verification method per device, OR a signed attestation that none happened

(2) is the real gap and not currently uploaded anywhere — confirmed by Drive search (no disposal log, asset register, or destruction-cert doc exists).

## Solution

### Recommended evidence artifact
Single Google Doc: **"Equipment Disposal & Re-use Log — [audit period]"**

Columns / fields:
- Device ID (asset tag or serial)
- Type (laptop / phone / external drive / etc.)
- Action (disposed / re-used internally / returned to leasing co.)
- Date
- Verification method (e.g. macOS *Erase All Content & Settings*, full OS reinstall, vendor destruction certificate ID)
- Responsible person
- Link to certificate / screenshot of wipe (if applicable)

If zero events occurred during the audit period: a one-paragraph attestation signed by Dennis/Kalle stating that no equipment containing storage media was disposed or re-used in the period, and that the procedure (Asset Mgmt Policy §X) would apply if any occurred.

Upload to **both** AST-71 (Kalle) and AST-1 (Dennis) as a Document evidence item.
