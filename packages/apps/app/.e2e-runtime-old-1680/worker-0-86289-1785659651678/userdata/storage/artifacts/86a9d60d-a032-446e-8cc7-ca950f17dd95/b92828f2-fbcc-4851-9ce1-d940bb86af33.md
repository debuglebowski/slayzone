# Subnet Flow Logs — Engineering Handoff

## Background

Vanta test **#15 "Subnets have flow logs enabled (Azure)"** is failing across **all 12 subnets** in subscription `Normain` (`a9049f9f-…`), Sweden Central:

- `vnet-normain-dev-20` — 6 subnets (`default`, `GatewaySubnet`, `snet-api`, `snet-db`, `snet-vm`, `snet-aks`)
- `vnet-normain-prod-01` — 6 subnets (same shape)

Today only **one** flow log exists (`nsg-vm-normain-prod-01`, NIC-targeted, 90 d retention). It does not satisfy the per-subnet check.

Why classic NSG flow logs can't fix this: 8 of the 12 subnets have no subnet-level NSG attached, and Microsoft has announced **NSG flow logs retire 2027-09-30**. Building more NSG flow logs is throwaway.

Policy constraints (Drive — authoritative):
- **Logging & Observability policy:** retention **minimum 180 days**, defined in IaC.
- **Data Management Policy:** flow logs contain IPs (PII) → Restricted, encryption at rest required, EU residency.

## Proposed Solution

Enable **VNet flow logs** — one per VNet, two total. Each one automatically covers every subnet in its VNet, so all 12 subnets are covered with two resources and no NSG changes.

- **Destination:** existing storage account `stsublogarch` (raw blobs) **plus** Traffic Analytics into the existing `law-normain-audit-dev-20` / `law-normain-audit-prod-01` workspaces.
- **Retention:** 180 days on the flow log resource to meet policy; storage lifecycle rule to tier blobs to Cool/Archive after 30 days for cost.
- **Rollout:** dev first, verify blobs + Traffic Analytics populate, then prod. Disable the legacy `nsg-vm-normain-prod-01` flow log once VNet flow logs are confirmed.
- **Codify in Terraform** alongside existing Azure Monitor IaC so retention and destination are auditable.
- **Estimated cost:** ~$15–40/month combined.

After enablement, trigger a Vanta rescan; test #15 should flip to passing within one cycle.
