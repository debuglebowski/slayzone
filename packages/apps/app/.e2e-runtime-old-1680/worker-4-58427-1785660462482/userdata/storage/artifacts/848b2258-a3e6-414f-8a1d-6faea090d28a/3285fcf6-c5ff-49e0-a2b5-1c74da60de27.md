# Creating a Private Offer in Microsoft Marketplace

Internal guide for creating and submitting a private offer to a customer via Partner Center.

## Prerequisites

- Product/plan published and live in Microsoft Marketplace
- Customer's **billing account ID** (they find this in Azure Portal → Cost Management + Billing → Properties)
- Terms & conditions PDF (if applicable)

## Steps

### 1. Open Partner Center

Go to **Partner Center** → **Marketplace offers** → **Private offers** → **+ Create private offer**

### 2. Customer Information

- **Billing account ID** — paste the customer's billing account ID (e.g. `5690809`). Required.
- **Description** — internal reference note (not visible to customer)

### 3. Customer Terms

- **Start date** — choose one:
  - *Accepted date* — pricing starts when customer accepts
  - *Specific month* — pricing starts on the 1st of a chosen month
- **End date** — select month/year. Offer available through the last day of that month.
- **Accept by date** — deadline (UTC) for customer to accept. After this the offer expires.

### 4. Terms & Conditions

Upload a PDF with custom terms. Must comply with Microsoft Publisher Agreement and supported billing models.

### 5. Customer Renewals

- *This is a renewal for an existing customer* — for renewals of current private offers or existing paid agreements
- *This is not a customer renewal*

### 6. Notification Contacts

Add up to 5 internal email addresses for status change notifications.

### 7. Prepared By

Enter the preparer's email. Customer sees this when reviewing. This person gets status notifications.

### 8. Product Offers

Click **+ Add offers/plans** to select up to 10 offers/plans. For each, configure:
- **Discount %** or **absolute price**

### 9. Review and Submit

Click **Review and submit**. After submission, Partner Center will prompt you to send an email notification to the customer — this email goes to whoever holds the billing account role (not a specific address we control).

## After Submission

1. **Send the customer the "Accepting the Private Offer" guide directly** — don't rely solely on the Partner Center email, as it goes to billing account role holders who may not be our contact person
2. Notify our contact person that the offer is ready and share the guide
3. Monitor status in Partner Center → Private offers dashboard
4. Status flow: Pending acceptance → Accepted → Purchased
