## Trap rows + asymmetry traps (catch fabricated or industry-confused answers)

- **Q15** is the explicit hallucination trap — neither company uses "carbon-negative" framing.
- **Q01, Q02, Q06, Q14**: industry-asymmetry traps. The "correct" answer for one company is "metric not applicable" or "reported differently" — a competitor that forces a one-size-fits-all numerical answer will be wrong on one side.
- **Q08**: subtle directional trap. H&M's cumulative trend is down (−24% vs 2019), but year-over-year direction in 2024 was slightly UP. A competitor that only looks at the multi-year reduction headline will say "decreased" when the precise 2024-vs-2023 answer is "slightly increased."
