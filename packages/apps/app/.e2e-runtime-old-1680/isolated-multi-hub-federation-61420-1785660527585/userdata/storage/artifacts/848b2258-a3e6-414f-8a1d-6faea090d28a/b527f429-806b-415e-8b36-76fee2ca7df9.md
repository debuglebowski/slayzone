# Getting Started — What We Need From You

Welcome! Before we can set up your offer, we need a few things from you.

## 1. Your Billing Account ID

This is a number that identifies your organization's billing setup in Azure. Here's how to find it:

1. Sign in to the [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com)
2. In the search bar at the top, type **Cost Management + Billing** and open it
3. Click **Settings** → **Properties** in the left menu
4. Copy your **Billing account ID**

## 2. Your Tenant ID

This identifies your organization's Azure directory. Here's how to find it:

1. In the [Azure Portal](https://portal.azure.com), search for **Microsoft Entra ID** (or **Azure Active Directory**) and open it
2. On the **Overview** page, copy your **Tenant ID**

## 3. Send Us This Information

Send your **Billing Account ID** and **Tenant ID** to your Normain contact. We'll take it from there.

## What Happens Next

1. We'll create the offer using the information you provided
2. While we do that, please review the **"Identifying the Right People"** guide to make sure the right people in your organization are ready for the next steps
3. When the offer is ready, we'll send you a link and a separate guide with step-by-step instructions to accept, purchase, and activate
