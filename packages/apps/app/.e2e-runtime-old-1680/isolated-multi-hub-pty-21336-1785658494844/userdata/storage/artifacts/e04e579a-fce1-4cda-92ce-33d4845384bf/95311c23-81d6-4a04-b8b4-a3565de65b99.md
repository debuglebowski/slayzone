# AI Provider Tycoon — Bankruptcy

Elimination condition.

## Decisions

### Threshold and treatment
- **Trigger:** `cash < 0` after end-of-tick burn (see [6. Burn Rate](asset:5e7773bc)).
- **No grace period** — eliminated immediately.
- **Assets vanish** — servers, in-progress research, brand all wiped. No salvage / NPC takeover.
- **Final ranking:** bankrupted players ranked by tick of elimination — later = better (you survived longer).

## Open

- Tiebreak when two players bankrupt on the same tick (suggest: by ending cash position, less negative wins).
- Whether published models keep generating revenue for nobody (probably no — they're just gone).
