# Discussion & Conclusions

The team worked through the scenario from report to containment and recovery, following the Incident Response Plan. The plan's stages were validated as broadly accurate and the incident was contained within the exercise hour. A few improvement areas were identified.

## What worked
- Reporting and coordination were clear — the Security Event Report, the #security thread, and the virtual huddle.
- Severity was assigned (P1) and the Security Delegate led the response.
- Triage → investigation → containment flowed: the stuck WAITING row was found via Bull Board, the database, and the worker logs, and cleared.
- The data-protection angle was assessed explicitly — no personal data processed or exposed, so no GDPR/DPA or IMY notification was triggered.

## Findings / areas to improve
- **The response plan can be simplified.** Too many steps and too much administration for our team size; the team followed it but with friction. Trim it so process never slows down the fix.
- **No proactive monitoring of stuck jobs.** We heard about the outage from customers, not from our own alerting — the clearest operational gap.
- **No runbook for clearing a stuck extraction row.** The fix was improvised; a short runbook would make it faster and lower-risk.

## Conclusions
- The Incident Response Plan is fundamentally sound and the team contained the incident within the hour.
- Highest-value improvements are to simplify the plan for speed, add alerting on stuck jobs, and write a runbook for clearing stuck rows.
